# Aura.fm Design Principles

## 1. The Archive is a character.
It isn't just software. It observes, reacts, and occasionally comments.

## 2. Show, don't dump.
Results should reveal themselves gradually through windows and interactions.

## 3. Metrics come before conclusions.
Every personality label must be backed by calculated behavioral vectors.

## 4. Monochrome first.
Color only appears after Chromatic Leakage.

## 5. Humor is earned.
The archive stays mostly clinical. Rare "PS:" notes and internal comments make the personality shine.

## 6. Every screen should feel explorable.
Users should discover files and reports instead of scrolling through one long page.

## 7. Style follows data.
Graphs, wallpaper, accents, and layout all adapt to the user's computed aura.
